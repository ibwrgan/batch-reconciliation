Batch Reconciliation

PO · WO · BOQ · GCL · PAC · POD — chain-of-custody verification

Upload any workbook containing your work-order, BOQ, handover, PAC, and dismantled-asset data — tab names and column headers don't need to match a fixed template. The tool reads every sheet's actual headers and figures out which sheet is which (and which column is which) from their content, so a renamed tab or reordered columns won't break it. It matches records by SiteID / TawalID / WONumber / ItemCode and flags anything that doesn't reconcile: BOQ totals vs WO amount, unit prices vs the contracted rate card, as-built vs design quantities, missing signatures (PAC, GCL sign-offs), dates that fall out of chronological order, and duplicate or cross-reused asset tags between BOQ and POD.

New: you're not limited to one tidy workbook — use Multiple files or Entire folder to drop in your raw WO/PAC/GCL/POD PDFs straight from a batch export (thousands of files is fine), and the tool reads and classifies them itself. xlsx BOQ/rate-card files in the same batch are picked up automatically too.
